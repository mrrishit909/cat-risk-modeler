Catastrophe Risk Modeler

An invented insurer's coastal book of 10,000 locations. A hurricane climate fitted to forty years of storms, a damage curve fitted to fifteen years of claims, 50,000 simulated landfalls through the policy terms and the treaty, exceedance curves, and a Category 4 stress test, every number set against the simulation truth. …
The coast, the book, the storms, the claims and the true hurricane climate are generated by a simulator in this repository; nothing is a real insurer, a real coastline or real hurricane data. The truth is shown only to judge the models, which never see it. No outside model or service is called.
